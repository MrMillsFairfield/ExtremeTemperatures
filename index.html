Antarctica Record

The coldest temperature ever recorded on Earth was -89°C at the Russian research station, Vostok in Antarctica. The average winter inland temperature in Antarctica is -59°C.

Siberian Record

The coldest temperature in the northern hemisphere was -78°C in Oimekon, Siberia. It is so cold that boiling water thrown into the air freezes before it hits the ground.

Space & Science

The coldest place in our solar system is probably Triton, a moon of the planet Neptune, where the temperature reaches -235°C.

The coldest temperature possible is -273°C, which is also called absolute zero. This is where atoms reach their lowest possible level of movement and everything freezes solid — even air. Scientists have used lab freezers and lasers to get within 100 billionths of a degree to absolute zero.

Scorching Heat

On the other extreme, the hottest temperature ever recorded on Earth was a blistering 57°C in Death Valley, California.

Dunedin Climate

In Dunedin, New Zealand, the climate is much milder. The average summer temperature is pleasant, but the hottest temperature ever recorded was 35°C, and the coldest temperature ever recorded was -8°C.
